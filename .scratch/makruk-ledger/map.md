# Map: Makruk Engine — Repair the Ledger

Labels: wayfinder:map
Status: **COMPLETE (2026-08-03)** — all six tickets resolved, no fog graduated into new ones.

**What the map delivered.** The ledger can now say *"this row's number stands, its description lied"*:
a **verified amendment** row (`results.mjs --amend`) that runs its own proof and refuses to write
without one, overlaid at read time so no row is ever edited. The five mis-recorded rows carry proven
amendments — **b0040/b0041 depth 5, b0042/b0043/b0044 depth 7** — and the audit fell from **15
contradictions to 4**, all four known and parked. The defect class is unwritable: `match-arena` builds
the row **once**, a schema (`lib/block-schema.mjs`) refuses a self-contradictory row at preflight and
quarantines one at write time rather than discarding a finished block, and `ledger-audit --gate` fails
on anything new against a ratcheting baseline. **`engineId`** pins the binary on both sides of every
block, so a proof means something. `node scripts/ledger-selftest.mjs` — 47 assertions.

**Two patches of fog were left un-graduated on purpose** (below). Neither is blocking; both are honest
"we do not know what the question is yet".
Predecessors: `../makruk-rig/map.md` (complete — built the ledger this map repairs), `../makruk-redraw/map.md` (live — parked the debt this map takes over)

## Destination

The ledger can say **"this row's number stands, its description lied"** — a verified amendment primitive that never touches an outcome and never lands without a reproduction proof. The map ends when the five mis-recorded rows carry proven amendments, a guard makes that defect class unwritable, and a block pins the engine that played it so a proof means something.

It exists because the ledger has exactly two ways to change the record — append a block, or retract one — and neither fits what actually went wrong. `b0040` and `b0043` are the load-bearing evidence for the redraw map's headline finding. They ran at `go depth 5` and `go depth 7`. They record `movetime: 100, opponentMovetime: 400`, which renders as *"the opponent had 4× the time and we still won."* Flattering, and false. Retracting them throws away sound measurements to fix a wrong field; editing them breaks the append-only rule the whole record rests on.

## Notes

- **Execution override — ON, with a cost ceiling.** Wayfinder's plan-only default is off: this map builds. A ticket closes when the change is in the repo with `cargo test --release` green. **Nothing here may cost more than ~5 minutes of machine time without a ticket that says so up front** — proof re-runs are seconds, and no ticket on this map needs a gate block, a corpus, or a training run.
- **Settled at charting (2026-08-03), not open for re-litigation.** Four decisions, grilled before any ticket existed:
  1. **Scope** — the correction primitive, the five rows, and a guard against recurrence. Not a rewrite of the record-keeping layer.
  2. **Primitive** — a **verified amendment row**, a new meta-type beside `retraction` and `clearance`. `readBlocks` overlays it; the original row is never touched.
  3. **What it may change** — **never the outcome.** An amendment corrects how a block was *run* (depth, movetime, seed, concurrency, armed eval). `games`/`w`/`l`/`d`/`score`/`perGame` are off-limits, enforced in code. If the *result* is untrustworthy that is a retraction; an amendment says the result stands and only its description was wrong.
  4. **Proof is mandatory.** No proof, no amendment — a row that cannot be reproduced can only be retracted. "Amended" and "verified" stay the same word, so a reader never has to ask which kind of amendment they are looking at.
- **Measured at charting — a fixed-depth block reproduces bit-exactly.** Re-ran `b0046` (depth 3) and `b0047` (depth 4) from their recorded seed on the current binary: **all 16 games matched tag-for-tag and ply-for-ply.** Then the two rows that matter: `b0040` re-runs at depth 5 as `DRAW/313 MAXPLY/400`, `b0043` at depth 7 as `MAXPLY/400 DRAW/237` — both exactly as recorded. **The claimed depths are proven, and the redraw map's "~25 min, therefore the user's call" blocker is refuted: two games and ~2 minutes settle a row.**
- **Ledger profile (2026-08-03): 49 blocks + 15 meta rows.** 5 fixed-depth (reproducible), **44 movetime (never bit-reproducible — timing jitter)**, **48 of 49 carry `engineDirty: true`**, 2 have no W/L/D recorded (`b0006`, `b0007`, both already retracted), 12 retractions, 3 clearances. All five mis-recorded rows are inside the reproducible five, so this repair is fully provable; the 44 are why decision 4 has teeth.
- **A proof assumes a binary the row cannot name.** The two proofs above are sound only because `src/` was separately confirmed untouched since those blocks ran. A future session cannot do that from the row. Pinning engine identity is therefore **on the route**, not a nicety — see [Pin the engine that played the block](issues/03-pin-the-engine-that-played.md).
- **The root cause is drift between two construction sites.** `identity` (what the control trigger fingerprints) and the `appendBlock` row are built independently from the same globals in `match-arena.mjs`, and had already diverged once — which is why redraw ticket 02's first patch appeared to do nothing. A guard that only validates one of them re-creates the bug.
- **Method rule, inherited and now 7-for-7:** audit the measurement before theorizing. It applies to this map's own work — during charting, the first three reproduction attempts "failed" because `FAIRY_BIN` was unset and the opponent was fairy's wasm at skill −20, not our classical eval. The ledger row caught it: it recorded the opponent honestly.
- Read results with `node scripts/results.mjs`, never hand-copy a number. `MAKURUK_LEDGER=<path>` redirects reads and writes to a scratch ledger — **use it for every experiment on this map**, so charting and design work never append to the committed record.
- Skills: `/grilling` + `/domain-modeling` on HITL tickets. No research tickets — nothing here needs knowledge from outside this repo.
- Refer to tickets by name in narration, never by number. Tracker: local markdown, this directory.

- [Which rows say something that cannot be true?](issues/01-audit-what-cannot-be-true.md) — **the five hold, and they are not the whole list.** `scripts/ledger-audit.mjs` sweeps 15 invariants over the record and finds **15 contradictions across 7 rows** in 3 classes. Search condition (b0040–b0044) is **amendable and needs exactly three fields** — `depth` set, `movetime`/`opponentMovetime` nulled — so the primitive's `set` vocabulary is already fixed by map decision 3, and two of the four invariants there (`control-time-symmetric`, `clearance-condition-matches`) are *consequences* that the same amendment clears. Convicted by **two independent detectors**, which the ticket did not ask for and needed: timing arithmetic alone is one-sided and **b0043 is 4% too SLOW, not too fast**, while paired determinism (b0044 replays 10/10 colour-reversed pairs at a claimed 100/400 ms) needs no threshold at all. **Decision 4 has a live consequence:** `b0010` is a *movetime* row that contradicts itself (`kind: gate-b`, `opponent.engine: ours`) and can never be reproduced — and its retraction reason, shared with b0012/b0013, reads *"the scores stand"*. **Three sound measurements were thrown out because retraction was the only tool**, and they are unprovable, so decision 4 refuses them; that makes ticket 02's awkward case #4 a question about real rows. **`armed` came back clean and is unfalsifiable** — `preflight.mjs` produces it by probing *our* binary under the *believed* env, so on b0010 it recorded "classic" for a side that was fairy, corroborating the error; only a per-side binary hash could have caught it, which is ticket 03's business. Suspect reasons on b0040/b0043/b0049 quote a mean pooled across search conditions but are **exactly reproducible**, so the text is faithful and the fault is the trigger's cell — **not amendable, leave it, let the audit flag it**.

- [The amendment record: shape, overlay, and what counts as a proof](issues/02-the-amendment-record.md) — **built, and it refuses a wrong claim on the row it was built for.** `results.mjs --amend` runs the proof itself (option A, the `clearSuspect` pattern) and appends only on a match: on b0040 against a scratch ledger, **depth 5 replayed `DRAW/313 MAXPLY/400` and landed; depth 6 replayed `DRAW/265` and was refused with nothing written** — the run that matters, because a verifier that never rejects looks identical to one that works. **Two games, widened when they say nothing:** games 0 and 1 are the same opening colour-reversed, so a wrong claim fails twice at once, but a slice whose games are indistinguishable (two stalls at the ply cap) is no evidence, and `proofWidth()` widens in whole pairs — computed from the *recorded* results, so it costs nothing. Because the audit proved `armed` is **unfalsifiable**, a mismatch **fails safe**: it reports that the replay did not reproduce, names the likelier cause first, and never declares the record wrong — charting itself lost three attempts to an unset `FAIRY_BIN`. Overlay is latest-wins per field with `amendedFrom` preserved, `table()` stacks `amended` beside `cleared by`, and **`ledger-audit.mjs` now overlays before it checks**, so ticket 05's prediction is already verified: an amended row's `timing-arithmetic` finding **disappears without being touched directly** while its un-amended twin stays convicted. Five new audit invariants police the row type, since `amend()` only guards the honest path. **b0010/b0012/b0013 stay refused by mechanism, not policy** — after any amendment they are still movetime blocks, so no proof exists. One design bug caught in itself: *retracted AND amended* is an ordinary history, not a contradiction; only an amendment written **after** a retraction is one. `node scripts/ledger-selftest.mjs`, 33 assertions.

- [Pin the engine that played the block](issues/03-pin-the-engine-that-played.md) — **`engineId` on both sides of every block: a content hash of the file that actually ran.** Our binary, the wasm fairy and native fairy all measured distinct on real blocks. Hashing the **binary** beats both alternatives for reasons already in the record — a source hash misses `+simd128` (43.7% nps, in no `.rs` file), and `evalinfo` is self-reported under the believed env. **Half the question was already answered:** our exporter and fairy both content-address their weights filenames, so `weights` *is* an identity; the missing half — fairy's `.nnue` — is now recorded too. **The opponent is pinned as well**, because every rung of the ladder is a measurement against fairy. One `sidesIdentity()` call spread into both construction sites (the ticket's Watch), living in `scripts/lib/identity.mjs`. **Deliberately absent from `fingerprint()`** — a rebuilt binary is the *artifact*, not new machinery, so including it would demand a control block after every `cargo build`. The proof side now resolves **same / different / unpinned before playing a game**: a mismatch refuses at zero cost, and all 49 historical rows are **`unpinned`** — they can never be pinned retroactively, so a proof records the binary it *actually ran against* rather than an equality nobody could check. The audit files that as **98 INCOMPLETE notes, contradictions unchanged at 15**; convicting the whole historical record would be a guard nobody keeps. `node scripts/ledger-selftest.mjs`, 37 assertions.

- [Make this defect class unwritable](issues/04-make-the-defect-unwritable.md) — **all three mechanisms, each at a different door, and the cause is gone.** `match-arena` no longer rebuilds the row from the same globals 340 lines after `identity` — the `appendBlock` row is now `{...identity}` plus what the games revealed, and `slotCount` reads `identity.concurrency` rather than recomputing it. Field-diffed against b0049: **zero fields lost.** The schema lives once in `scripts/lib/block-schema.mjs`, 11 rules tagged `pre` (checked in preflight, where refusing is free) or `write`. **One line shapes the whole file: every rule is "if X and Y are both present they must agree" — a missing field is never a violation**, which is the ticket's Watch turned into a design rule and is why, of 49 historical rows, **the schema convicts exactly one** (b0010, a real contradiction) while all 44 movetime rows and both W/L/D-less rows pass — asserted against the committed ledger. Write-time refusal is a **quarantine, not a discard**: a failing row goes to `results/rejected.jsonl` with its violations and burns no id, because a throw at `appendBlock` costs a finished 23-minute block. The audit is now a **gate with a baseline** (`results/audit-baseline.json`, 15 entries) failing only on something new — the way a linter is adopted on a record that predates it, and **12 of the 15 are b0040–b0044**, so [Amend the five](issues/05-amend-the-five-and-reconcile.md) inherits a work-list with a number on it. `node scripts/ledger-selftest.mjs`, 43 assertions.

- [Amend the five, and reconcile everything that quotes them](issues/05-amend-the-five-and-reconcile.md) — **all five amended, each with a proof; 49 block rows, zero edited.** b0040/b0041 at depth 5, b0042/b0043/b0044 at depth 7. The audit fell **15 contradictions → 4**, and the prediction held: **11 stopped firing, including `clearance-condition-matches` and `control-time-symmetric`, none touched directly** — the overlay re-derives instead of caching. The **auto-widening earned its place on first real use**: b0041 and b0044 both open with two identical ply counts, and the extra pair they widened to (`FAIRY/106 MINE/106`, `DRAW/286 DRAW/286` — colour-reversed pairs replaying mirrored) is the strongest evidence in the set. **b0042 was depth 7, not the predicted 6**, and both theories that said otherwise were refuted by replay: the s/game bracket ignored that **per-game cost scales with a block's concurrency** (b0042 ran 4 games at 4, b0043 47 at 6), and the "0.707 timing ratio means it was really movetime" reading — tempting, because the audit's 0.75 floor was drawn *to convict b0042* — was simply wrong. Threshold-drawn-to-fit is a real smell and it still misled; that is the argument for a primitive that verifies rather than reasons. Reconciled outward: standings regenerated, AGENTS.md prose swept (**nothing quoted these conditions in running text** — the generated table doing its job), and both redraw artifacts now point at `results/blocks.jsonl` as the source instead of holding it themselves. Baseline ratcheted to **4**, all parked. `node scripts/ledger-selftest.mjs`, 43 assertions.

- [Should a control block appear in the standings?](issues/06-controls-in-the-standings.md) — **no. The standings went 21 rows → 13, and all 8 controls pass.** Not because b0009 is broken — it passes its band — but because a self-play control's expected score is **0.5 by construction**, so a score column beside a Gate A verdict can only mislead: `net 4452 | net 4452 | 40.6%` scans as the incumbent losing badly and the opponent was itself. Hiding it costs something the smoke/diag exclusion does not, since a control is *evidence about* the standings, so the footer carries the **verdict rather than the count** — `8 controls, all passing`, and on failure `N controls, 1 FAILING` plus the block id and `controlVerdict()`'s text. Both branches tested through the CLI, because the decision lives in what a reader sees. **The two `NOISE_KINDS` sets stay separate, and the reason got sharper:** after this change they have *identical members for different reasons* — the most dangerous shape a pair of constants can take. Renamed to **`NOT_A_RESULT`** (editorial: a reader would misread it) and **`NOT_TREND_EVIDENCE`** (mechanical: a 0.5-by-construction block poisons `rung()`'s trend), each naming the other and saying why it is not shared. The one place on this map where the answer to "two things built from the same idea" is **keep them apart** — they were never the same idea.

## Out of scope

- **Clause (a)'s cell ignoring search conditions.** `control-trigger.mjs`'s `rung()` pools blocks for an (artifact, opponent) pair regardless of depth, so it will fire clause (a) on essentially every depth block forever. Real, and explicitly ruled out here: it is control-trigger *behaviour*, not the ledger *record*. Stays parked in the redraw map's fog — this map does not swallow it. Confirmed live by [the audit](issues/01-audit-what-cannot-be-true.md): all three suspect reasons quote a mean pooled across search conditions, and all three are exactly reproducible, so the fault is the cell and not the record.
- **`observedSd` drops max-plies games, widening every control band by up to 45%.** It computes the per-game sd over `w + l + d` while the score is computed over `w + l + d + maxPlies` (`control-trigger.mjs:41`), discarding the least-dispersed observations and shrinking n. Measured on b0048: **±14.9pp shipped vs ±10.3pp** including them; b0041 ±13.5 vs ±10.3. **No verdict on today's ledger flips**, so it has cost nothing yet, but it biases every control toward "the plumbing is fine". Same boundary as clause (a) — trigger behaviour, not the record — and parked in the same place. Named here rather than left silent because `scripts/ledger-audit.mjs` calls `controlVerdict()` and therefore inherits the bias.

- **Re-running the five blocks for a fresh measurement.** The whole premise is that those measurements stand. A re-run appears here only as *proof*, never as a replacement number.
