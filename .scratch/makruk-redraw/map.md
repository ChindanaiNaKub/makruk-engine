# Map: Makruk Engine — The Strength Redraw

Labels: wayfinder:map
Status: charting (2026-08-02)

## Destination

A strength target for this engine **stated on the fairy skill 5–10 ladder**, with **measured evidence that it is reachable**, and the **lever chosen to reach it**. The map ends when §0 of `docs/strength-spec-v1.md` can be rewritten with a number nothing in the record contradicts, and the next build session knows exactly what to work on.

It replaces a target that died twice over: "≥50% vs fairy skill 20, in-browser, equal time" has no surviving evidence it is reachable (skill 10 and 20 return the identical 0–64–0, so they are one gate, not two rungs), and the vehicle it named — a distilled net — is measurably **behind** the classical eval it was built to replace.

## Notes

- **Execution override — the cheap half only.** Wayfinder's plan-only default is partially OFF. Any ticket may run arena blocks, depth/nps/eval probes, and `src/` changes; a ticket closes when the change is in the repo with `cargo test --release` + mirror-perft green. **A training sweep or a new corpus is NOT covered** — those need their own ticket that states wall-clock and lockout cost up front, decided by the user. Nothing in this map may cost more than ~20 min of machine lockout without that ticket.
- **The yardstick is fairy, restricted to its live rungs.** Skill 3 is a floor both artifacts clear (classic 72.7%, net 71.9% — inside one SE). Skill 5 and 8 resolve (34.4%, 15.6%). Skill 10, 15 and 20 are one indistinguishable wall. The target lives on **5, 8 or 10** and nowhere else. Gate A (the head-to-head SPRT) stays the *selection* mechanism — it is rig machinery, not the target.
- **Appetite: thermally cheap, open-ended.** The binding constraint is the machine, not the calendar. The target lands wherever the free levers can be *shown* to reach, and the map says honestly where that is. 2026-08-02 cost the user ~10 hours of lockout; that does not happen again.
- **Method rule, now 6-for-6:** an unexpected *negative* result is a reason to audit the measurement before theorizing about the engine. Every one of six harness defects made the engine look worse than it was. **This map opens by applying that rule to the biggest negative result in the record** — "classic beats the net" was measured with the net at a 3-ply handicap, and has never been audited for it.
- **Never quote an SPRT point estimate as an effect size** — it stops when ahead, so it is biased away from the boundary. Use a fixed-N block to size an effect; the SPRT is for the decision.
- All results live in `results/blocks.jsonl`. Read them with `node scripts/results.mjs`, never hand-copy a number. The rig auto-runs control blocks on trigger and marks suspect rows — trust the ledger, not a transcript.
- **Ledger debt — PAID 2026-08-03 by [Repair the Ledger](../makruk-ledger/map.md).** All five rows (b0040–b0044) now read their true search condition, each carrying a proof that the claimed depth reproduces the recorded games: **b0040 and b0041 depth 5, b0042/b0043/b0044 depth 7.** b0042's depth was recorded nowhere and had to be *found* — the bracketing guess of 6 was wrong, because per-game cost scales with a block's concurrency. Nothing was retracted and no row was edited: an **amendment** is a new meta-row overlaid at read time, so the numbers stand and only the descriptions changed. `node scripts/results.mjs` now renders these as `depth 5` / `depth 7` rather than *"the opponent had 4× the time"*, and the ledger audit fell from **15 contradictions to 4**. **This ticket is therefore no longer the sole source of the true conditions** — the ledger is. The verbatim commands below are kept as history, not as the record.
- Skills: `/grilling` + `/domain-modeling` on HITL tickets; `/research` subagent on research tickets.

- [Is "classic beats the net" a speed artifact?](issues/02-is-classic-beating-the-net-a-speed-artifact.md) — **yes, and the NNUE program is alive.** At equal search depth the net's eval *beats* the classical eval: **44.2%** at equal movetime (net depth ~5 vs classic ~8) → **54.2%** at equal depth 5 → **71.9%** at depth 6 → **67.0%** at depth 7 (SPRT ACCEPT, LLR 5.64). **Across depths 6–7 the net went 23–0 in decisive games over 63 games.** Controls clean at every depth tested (50.0% at 5, 6 and 7), each run at its own matching depth. Not a smooth gradient — the 6-vs-7 ordering is inside noise at n=16 — but a clear step from "behind at a 3-ply handicap" to "decisively ahead at parity." The recorded refutation measured *eval quality plus eval speed* and attributed all of it to quality — the seventh time a measurement, not the engine, was the problem. It does **not** establish the net wins as shipped: fixed depth is not playable, and the whole map now turns on [How much of the net's speed deficit can the accumulator close?](issues/08-how-much-speed-can-the-accumulator-close.md), which this graduates out of the fog. Also exposed a ledger defect — the first fixed-depth block ever run recorded `movetime 100/400` and no depth, rendering as "the opponent had 4× time and we still won." Patched forward here; the five rows already written were repaired by [Repair the Ledger](../makruk-ledger/map.md) on 2026-08-03 and now read their proven depths.

- [What are the missing search and eval techniques actually worth?](issues/03-what-the-missing-techniques-are-worth.md) — **16 techniques priced, and the ticket was wrong about three things.** Check extensions are **already implemented** (`src/search.rs:271`). The biggest eval finding was not on the list: **`counting_term()` returns 0 while `game.counting` is `None`**, so the eval is blind to a count *approaching* — at a 400–700 cp edge with 4–8 pieces, the stronger side scores **92.3% while any unpromoted bia remains and 62.0% once they are gone**, a 30-point swing with no chess analogue. Recommendation: **build the eval tuner first** — retuning the shipped constants improves held-out loss **−4.08%**, meaning the positional terms currently predict results *worse than deleting them*, at zero nps cost, and it is the instrument that prices every other eval row. **Its own headline result was then refuted by a peer sweep and the refutation holds:** `src/eval.rs:72` is `Kind::M | Kind::PM => 200` — met and promoted bia are the same piece — and the fit split them **112 vs 96**, per-phase **168 vs 8**. That is parameter unidentifiability (Muller: balanced-material corpora cannot separate piece values), confirmed by rua moving 500 → **652**, *toward* the published band where Fairy-Max/SjaakII/Makruk-Stockfish agree. **The −4.08% and "tune first" survive; every per-piece number does not** — and with it the compressed-values argument that was the sole reason SEE ranked 15th. **A third pass then found the makruk literature the ticket assumed did not exist**, and it moved the table again (18 rows now, with a `[K]` class — *measured by someone else, on makruk*). **The one makruk-measured Elo figure anywhere: +18** (Glebbeek/SjaakII) for replacing `if (in check) depth++` with an **SEE-gated** extension — `src/search.rs:271` is that naive form verbatim, but `grep -rn "SEE" src/*.rs` returns only zobrist `SEED`, so **it is unreachable until SEE is written, which raises SEE's value, not the extension's.** **The TT advice was retracted by its own author:** 885k nps × 100 ms ≈ 88,500 nodes/move against `TT_SIZE = 1 << 18` = 262,144 — the table is **3× larger than the entire tree for a move** where Muller's rule needs it 10× smaller, so aging *and* replacement policy both measure ~0 within a search; TT aging fell 5th → **14th**. **Four published makruk piece tables** now check the fit: rua 652 lands inside the published **625–676**, met 112 is below every source (consensus **150–192**), and **the shipped table is the outlier of the set** — highest met, lowest rua, because it is the counting-rule adjudication table pressed into service as a playing-strength table. The identifiability failure is a **documented trap** (hgm/jdart), so **split the tuning job**: positional terms on `bootstrap-v2`, piece values not from this fit. And **lower loss is not Elo** — Zurichess measured **−28 Elo despite a lower MSE** — which is exactly this ticket's own evidence, so nothing ships without Gate A. Also settled: **ablation ≠ addition Elo** (RFP is +57 added, −32 ablated); model gates on **Ethereal, not Stockfish**; **PVS is an ordering amplifier** (~+55 twice, but −12.7 under a buggy move scorer — verify ordering first); **Makruk-Stockfish *scales* the win score by remaining count** rather than adding an offset, which is the right shape for our counting term. Graduated [Is the eval tuner a trustworthy instrument?](issues/09-is-the-eval-tuner-trustworthy.md) out of the fog.

- [Is the eval tuner a trustworthy instrument?](issues/09-is-the-eval-tuner-trustworthy.md) — **yes for pricing eval terms, no as a source of piece constants — and the pre-stated test compared the wrong quantity.** `training/tune_eval.py`, ~4 min of one core. **The instrument was validated before it was used:** `--verify` scores real corpus positions against the engine's own UCI `eval` — **373/400 exact, 27 off by exactly −50**, the in-check bonus it deliberately omits, zero unexplained deltas. Under the three constraints (bia anchored 100, met tied to promoted bia, by-game split) held-out loss goes **0.085930 → 0.082454, −4.05%**, independently reproducing ticket 03's −4.08%. **The literal test FAILED**: bare met **143.2**, outside 150–192 (the unconstrained fit said 112, so the constraints moved most of the way). **But the bootstrap refuted the failure branch's stated reason** — 10 resamples of the training *games* put met at **141–146, width 5** against a published inter-engine disagreement of 33, so the corpus identifies material *precisely*. The real diagnosis is **collinearity**, and `ma` proves it without reference to the met: its material constant moved 300 → 284 while its **effective value moved −1.6%**, the fit having shifted the value into the knight's centre terms. On the like-for-like comparison — total contribution, which is what published material-only tables from engines with their own PSTs actually correspond to — **met is 160.4, inside the band**. So: price eval terms against held-out loss, never read `kind_value()` out of the fit, and ship nothing on loss alone (Zurichess: **−28 Elo at a lower MSE**). **Handed to [05](issues/05-what-one-eval-term-is-worth.md): the king-safety table may have the wrong sign** — shipped rewards the back-rank corners, the fit drives `king10` to **−41** and `king20` to **−47** — and the bia advance bonus wants its sign reversed on the reachable rows. Both cost one Gate A block to settle.

- [What is one classical-eval improvement actually worth?](issues/05-what-one-eval-term-is-worth.md) — **REJECTED, and the null is the finding: lower held-out loss is not Elo, now demonstrated on THIS engine.** The tuner's best-evidenced candidate — the two counting multipliers, **−0.82% held out**, more than any other pair in the eval and 5× the king-safety table's four constants — went to Gate A at equal time and lost: **b0054 48.8% (−9 Elo), LLR −9.38, REJECT at 20 pairs**, against a clean **50.0%** control (b0053). Zero nps cost, provably (same instruction, different immediate). Reverted; the rebuilt binary reproduces the incumbent bit-exactly. [Ticket 09](issues/09-is-the-eval-tuner-trustworthy.md) warned of this citing Zurichess (−28 Elo at lower MSE) — **that was borrowed, this is first-party, and it landed on the strongest signal the tuner produced.** The tuner is not disqualified (its verdict was already "prices terms, does not decide them") but its ranking is now a known-weak predictor of Elo at the *top* end, not just the margins. **King safety — the path this ticket was opened on — died for free**: isolated it is 5th of 6 at −0.16%, and its table is a *scramble* (`king5` +25, `king10` −28, `king15` +3, `king20` 0) that disagrees with the joint fit's −41/−47, so those four constants are not independently identified. Material carries 75% of the joint gain and stays unshippable per ticket 09's collinearity finding. **Three rig gaps surfaced, one root cause — "the opponent is fairy"** — because *every* Gate A in this ledger's history is net-vs-classic, differentiated by eval, never by build: `oppIsOurs` recorded a second build as fairy (fixed with **`OPP_BIN`**; the schema guard caught it), `sideIdentity` ignored the binary so preflight refused a legitimate block, and **`FAIRYTIME` defaulted to 4×, costing a whole block** — b0051 retracted at −26 Elo measuring nothing but a clock handicap, while the auto-control path had forced equal time all along. **Consequence for [Set the target](issues/07-set-the-target.md): do not assume the classical eval has cheap headroom** — its strongest ranked candidate returned negative and its second died on free evidence.

## Not yet specified

- **Clause (a)'s "cell" ignores search conditions.** `control-trigger.mjs`'s `rung()` pools every block for an (artifact, opponent) pair regardless of depth, so it read "55.1% previously measured at this exact cell" by averaging a movetime block with two fixed-depth ones, and will now fire clause (a) on essentially every depth block forever — alarm fatigue on a check that exists to catch real deviations. Deliberately **not** fixed here: the fingerprint fix was one declared exception and a second would be redesign. **Still parked as of 2026-08-03** — [Repair the Ledger](../makruk-ledger/map.md) explicitly ruled it out of scope (it is control-trigger *behaviour*, not the ledger *record*), so it did not travel with the ledger debt and remains this map's fog. See [Is "classic beats the net" a speed artifact?](issues/02-is-classic-beating-the-net-a-speed-artifact.md).
- **Is the net's edge general, or concentrated in counting endgames?** The depth-7 games ran long and counting-heavy (many 220–330 plies ending in `counting_rule`; net-vs-net produced 10 max-plies in 20 games). The net carries ~10 counting side-channels into its tail; classic has one hand-written counting term. If the advantage is concentrated there rather than general, the lever changes — a counting term in the classical eval might buy most of it for a fraction of the cost. **Sharper as of 2026-08-03:** [What are the missing techniques worth?](issues/03-what-the-missing-techniques-are-worth.md) measured the gap the classical eval has there — `counting_term()` is blind to a count *approaching*, a 30-point swing — and found that **Makruk-Stockfish scales the win score by remaining count** where ours adds a fixed offset. So the "cheap counting term" hypothesis now has both a measured size and a working template. Still fog because the *comparison* it turns on — how much of the net's edge is counting-specific — is unmeasured. Sharpen once the speed question resolves.
- **Native vs in-browser.** The old §0 stated its claim on *in-browser wasm*; every measurement in the record is **native**. Whether a native target transfers, and whether §7's never-run `nps ≥ 500k` wasm gate belongs in this map at all, is undecided. Sharpen at [Set the target](issues/07-set-the-target.md) — it cannot be dodged there, because the target's wording depends on it.
- **Corpus composition** — inherited from the rig map's Out of scope. The measured handoff: DAgger corpora are ~47% opening / ~41% middlegame with **zero** deep endgames (0.0% bare vs the bootstrap corpus's 30.4%), so the spec's "rebalance toward opening/middlegame" recommendation describes a lever already pulled to its limit for no measurable gain. Only fair game if the net survives **and** a costed training ticket is earned.
- **KMITL 2026 endgame tablebases.** Spec §6's frozen probe set from the retrograde bases was never built, and the bases are ground truth. Might be a cheap accuracy diagnostic, might be a training target, might be a direct endgame oracle in search. Three different tickets hiding in one patch; sharpen once the lever is chosen. **Now citable:** Tudsuan & Thanatipanonda (2026), *"Building Makruk Endgame Tablebases"* — the 44-move pieces'-honour limit drops khon + promoted-bia vs bare khun from **85.77% to 83.49%**, eliminating 278,040 positions needing more than 41 moves, including mates up to 57 long. Independent ground-truth confirmation that counting materially changes endgame outcomes.

- **An imbalance-seeded corpus for piece values.** The only correct way to fit material, per hgm's prescription: self-play from deliberately imbalanced starting positions, with piece values fit on that corpus alone. `match-arena.mjs` can already dump games, so the machinery is close — but seeding imbalanced starts is **new datagen**, which the map's standing rule sends to its own costed ticket stating wall-clock and lockout up front. Only fair game if [Is the eval tuner a trustworthy instrument?](issues/09-is-the-eval-tuner-trustworthy.md) returns "not ready" *and* the piece values turn out to be load-bearing for the target.
- **Conversion failure.** Our engines abort **25%** of games against each other at the 400-ply cap and ~3% against fairy — fairy converts won positions, we do not. Against fairy at the live rungs this barely registers, so it may be pure Gate A noise; or it may be a real endgame weakness that the ladder hides. Unclear whether it is an eval, search, or counting-rule question.
- **Time management.** Every arena block is fixed-movetime, so time allocation is invisible to every measurement ever taken here — but real play on the site is not fixed-movetime. Entirely unpriced.
- **Is "beat fairy" the right frame at all?** Fairy below skill 20 is a depth-10 engine deliberately throwing moves away, which is an odd thing to aim at. Deferred rather than dismissed: the yardstick is chosen *for this map*, and this patch is the note that the choice was made under uncertainty.
